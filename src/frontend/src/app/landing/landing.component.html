Built for every field

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Any type of work
Submit essays, code, design files, case studies, lab reports, or presentations. Bonus scoring rewards work that exceeds the brief.
Calibrated reviews
Reviewer quality is tracked. Evaluators who score unfairly or inconsistently lose review privileges — protecting every submission.
Threshold-based passing
Each institution sets its own passing bar. Students advance on merit, at their own pace, with no arbitrary deadlines.
Group projects
Form ad-hoc teams for collaborative work. Shared submissions with individual reviewer feedback for every contributor.
Institute panel
Schools and universities define programmes, set thresholds, and monitor entire cohorts from a single dashboard. No grading. Pure oversight.
Transparent progress
Every score, every reviewer, every assignment — fully visible on the student's profile. No hidden rubrics. No surprises.
